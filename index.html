140 weavers, one loom at a time

When Almaz Tesfaye moved the Gojo Collective onto 4KILO in 2021, forty women were weaving. Payouts arrived every Friday instead of every season, and the cooperative could finally buy wool in bulk. Today it is a hundred and forty.
